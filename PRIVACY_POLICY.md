# Privacy Policy for Emergency Button - SOS App

**Effective Date**: September 18, 2024  
**Last Updated**: September 18, 2024

## Introduction

Emergency Button - SOS App ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our emergency alert application.

## Information We Collect

### Contact Information
- **What we collect**: Names and phone numbers of your emergency contacts
- **Why we collect it**: To send emergency alerts and make emergency calls
- **How we collect it**: You manually add contacts through the app's settings
- **Storage**: All contact information is stored locally on your device

### Location Information
- **What we collect**: Your current GPS location when you activate an emergency alert
- **Why we collect it**: To include your location in emergency messages sent to your contacts
- **How we collect it**: Only when you press the emergency button
- **Storage**: Location data is not stored permanently; it's only used for the current emergency alert

### App Usage Data
- **What we collect**: Basic app functionality data (emergency button presses, settings preferences)
- **Why we collect it**: To ensure the app functions properly during emergencies
- **How we collect it**: Automatically when you use the app
- **Storage**: Stored locally on your device

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:

1. **Emergency Alerts**: To send SMS messages and make phone calls to your designated emergency contacts
2. **Location Sharing**: To include your current location in emergency messages
3. **App Functionality**: To maintain your emergency contact list and app settings
4. **Safety Features**: To provide vibration alerts and sound notifications

## Information Sharing

**We do NOT share, sell, or distribute your personal information to any third parties.**

Your information is used exclusively for emergency purposes and is only shared with:
- Your designated emergency contacts (when you activate an emergency alert)
- Emergency services (if you choose to call them directly)

## Data Storage and Security

### Local Storage
- All your data is stored locally on your device
- We do not store your information on external servers
- Your emergency contacts and settings remain private to you

### Security Measures
- Data is encrypted using Android's built-in encryption
- No data is transmitted to external servers
- Your privacy is protected by design

## Your Rights and Choices

### Access and Control
- You can view, edit, or delete your emergency contacts at any time
- You can disable location sharing in your device settings
- You can uninstall the app to remove all data

### Emergency Contact Management
- Add or remove emergency contacts through the app settings
- Update contact information as needed
- Test emergency functionality safely

## Third-Party Services

Our app uses the following Android system services:
- **SMS Service**: To send emergency text messages
- **Phone Service**: To make emergency calls
- **Location Services**: To get your current location
- **Vibration Service**: To provide haptic feedback

These services are provided by your device's operating system and are subject to their respective privacy policies.

## Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for general use and does not specifically target children under 13. However, if you are under 13, please ensure you have parental consent before using this app.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app
- Sending a notification through the app (if significant changes)

## Emergency Disclaimer

- **Location Data**: Not stored permanently; used only for current emergency alerts
- **App Settings**: Stored until you change them or uninstall the app
