**Emergency Button - Your Personal Safety Companion**

Send emergency alerts with your location to your emergency contacts with just one tap. This life-saving app ensures help is always just a button press away.

**🚨 Key Features:**
• One-tap emergency alerts
• GPS location sharing
• SMS and phone call notifications
• Floating SOS button
• Emergency contacts management
• Quick access widget
• Material Design 3 interface

**🛡️ Safety & Privacy:**
• Local data storage - your info never leaves your device
• Encrypted storage for sensitive data
• Privacy-first approach
• No cloud dependencies

**🎯 Perfect For:**
• Personal safety
• Family emergency communication
• Elderly care
• Travel safety
• Workplace emergencies

**⚡ Quick Setup:**
1. Add emergency contacts
2. Configure notification options
3. Test in safe environment
4. Enable floating SOS button

**🔧 Requirements:**
• Android 7.0+
• Location, SMS, Phone, Contacts permissions
• 50MB storage space

**⚠️ Important:**
This app supplements emergency services. For real emergencies, call 911, 112, or your local emergency number.
