## 🎯 **What's New in This Release**

### 🆕 **Core Features**
- **One-Tap Emergency Alerts**: Send emergency notifications with just a single tap
- **Multi-Channel Notifications**: Choose between SMS, phone calls, or both
- **GPS Location Sharing**: Automatically include your current location in emergency messages
- **Floating SOS Button**: Always-accessible emergency button that floats over other apps
- **Emergency Contacts Management**: Easily manage and organize your emergency contacts
- **Quick Access Widget**: Emergency button directly on your home screen

### 🔧 **Technical Features**
- **Smart Permission Management**: Streamlined permission requests for optimal user experience
- **Background Service**: Reliable emergency service that works even when app is closed
- **Battery Optimized**: Efficient power usage to ensure app is always ready
- **Material Design 3**: Modern, intuitive user interface following Google's design guidelines
- **Dark/Light Theme Support**: Automatic theme adaptation based on system settings

## 🚀 **Getting Started**

### 📥 **Installation**
1. Download from Google Play Store
2. Grant necessary permissions when prompted
3. Add your emergency contacts in Settings
4. Test the emergency button in a safe environment

### ⚙️ **Initial Setup**
1. **Add Emergency Contacts**: Go to Settings → Emergency Contacts
2. **Configure Emergency Options**: Choose SMS, Call, or both
3. **Enable Floating SOS**: Optional always-accessible emergency button
4. **Test Emergency Button**: Verify everything works correctly

---

## 🆘 **How to Use in Emergency**

### 🚨 **Emergency Activation**
1. **Press the Red Emergency Button** on the main screen
2. **Wait for 5-second countdown** (or press Cancel if accidental)
3. **Emergency alert is sent** to all configured contacts
4. **Location is automatically included** in the message

### 📱 **Floating SOS Button**
- **Enable in Settings** for always-accessible emergency access
- **Floats over other apps** for quick access
- **Same emergency functionality** as main button

## 🐛 **Known Issues & Limitations**

### ⚠️ **Current Limitations**
- **SMS Delivery**: SMS delivery depends on carrier and network conditions
- **Location Accuracy**: GPS accuracy may vary based on device and environment
- **Battery Usage**: Continuous location services may impact battery life
- **Network Dependency**: Emergency features require mobile network connection

### 🔧 **Troubleshooting**
- **Permission Issues**: Ensure all permissions are granted in Settings
- **Location Not Working**: Check GPS settings and location permissions
- **SMS Not Sending**: Verify SMS permissions and carrier settings
- **Floating Button Not Appearing**: Enable "Display over other apps" permission
